Team 26 built EduPulse, which monitors online course materials so students and staff learn when a website, lecture recording, or assignment link becomes unavailable or misses its scheduled release. Instructors and teaching assistants register resources; background workers check them automatically, confirm outages only after repeated failures, and email course members when a problem starts or ends. Students follow status on a live dashboard and can report an issue to trigger an immediate recheck. EduPulse runs containerized FastAPI and worker services on DigitalOcean with Docker Swarm, PostgreSQL on a persistent volume, WebSocket updates, and provider monitoring and alerts.
